[1] HOW TO PLAY
- L-Click: Build Turret (50G) / R-Click: Sell

- Build 3 turrets of same type/tier to auto-merge!
- 1-Star x3 -> 2-Star (+)
- 2-Star x3 -> 3-Star (*)

[3] UPGRADES
- [Z/X/C] Upg Flame/Ice/Storm (100G): +1 Damage!
- [R] Random Upgrade (80G): Random +1 Damage!

[4] SLOT MACHINE
- [T] Gamble (300G) -> Roll 3 dice (1~6).
- Reward: (D1 x D2 x D3) x 10 Gold! (Max 2160G!)
